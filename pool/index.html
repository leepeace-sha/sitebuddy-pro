How to play Aim: touch / drag anywhere on the table. Fine aim: drag the wheel on the right. Shoot: pull the power bar on the left down and let go. Spin: tap the cue ball top-right. Ball in hand: drag the cue ball. On the 8-ball, tap a pocket to call it.
