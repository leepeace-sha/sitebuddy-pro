Clubhouse 8-Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Clubhouse 8-Ball does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The Android app does not request internet access. It cannot send data anywhere.
  • Your settings (sound, aim guide, cloth and cue choice) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The app uses vibration for short haptic feedback when a ball is potted.

The game is suitable for all ages and contains no gambling or real-money features.

Questions: contact the developer through the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
